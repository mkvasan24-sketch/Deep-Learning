Important Model Architecture & Dataset Scope

The underlying Keras model features a Flatten(32×32×3) input layer mapped through three dense ReLU layers (312, 256, 128 units) to a 10-way Softmax output. It is purpose-built to recognize the 10 classes above. Real-world images containing objects outside these 10 categories will be mapped to the closest mathematical representation among these classes.
